Style teardown — ten products, measured live

Computed-style extraction from OpenAI Platform, Claude Console, Twenty, Evernote, Monday, Pipedrive, Linear, Intercom, ElevenLabs and Manus. Swatches and numbers are literal values read from the DOM; each specimen is rebuilt from those tokens (typefaces approximated by system stacks). Dark-mode strips were measured by flipping each product into its own dark theme and restoring it after. Companion page: app shell taxonomy — the conceptual teardown of layout archetypes.

How they name their tokens

Evernote—--color-{component}-{element}-fill-{state} (1,900+!)component-scoped—
Monday—--primary-text-color, --text-color-on-primary--font-size-10…60 + h1…h5--space-2…80 (px-named)
Pipedrive--pd-compiled away——
Linear—--color-text-primary…quaternarymicro/microPlus…large/largePlus/title1-3; fonts by rolenone public
IntercomTW classestext-default, text-muted, bg-neutral-containertext-sm/h1TW + gap-module, shadow-lvl-0
ElevenLabs—--gray-alpha-{step}; --{status}-text/bg--font-waldenburgTW --spacing; --radius-xs…
Manus—--text-primary/secondary/tertiary/onblack—raw px literals
  • Rank naming won: primary / secondary / tertiary (/ quaternary) is the majority text-color scheme — exactly our --text-* convention. Tail ranks get words (muted, disabled, extra-light), not numbers.
  • Two philosophies on states: OpenAI + Evernote bake style and state into names (1,000–1,900 tokens); Linear, Manus and Claude keep names small and resolve states elsewhere — the camp our "recipes, not tokens" rule is in.
  • Spacing schools: numbered-steps × base (Twenty, Tailwind), value-named px (Monday's --space-8), t-shirt (--cds-pad-md), or none at all (Linear, Manus).
  • Most elegant type naming — Linear: families by role (--font-display), sizes as a word scale with codified half-steps (small/smallPlus).

  • Alpha borders beat solid grays. OpenAI, Claude and Pipedrive define borders as text-color at 10–14% opacity — one token that adapts to every surface and to dark mode.
  • Hover is an overlay, not a color. Black 4–5% at OpenAI, Claude, Twenty — matches our "recipes, not tokens" rule.
  • Nobody ships real elevation on light UI. Four of six have zero shadows; separation = tint + hairline. Validates the flat-dense finish.
  • One accent does the work; extra hues are demoted to chips and pastel data colors.
  • Warm gray = paper products (Claude, Evernote), cool/neutral = tools — temperature is brand work, same as pegbo-warm vs pegbo-emerald.
  • Weight replaces size at small scales — Twenty's one-size 13px, Pipedrive's wght-450 body and Linear's 13px-w500 chrome all carry hierarchy with weight only. Weight 450 appears independently at Pipedrive and Linear.
  • Card radius ≈ control radius + 4 in every single product — the same relationship as our 7/13/19 scale.
  • The floating-panel frame (Twenty, Intercom, Linear): tinted canvas + white panels lifted by a 1px ring and whisper shadow — structure without borders or heavy elevation.
  • Tinted canvas as brand — Intercom's olive #eff0eb and Linear's hue-282 grays carry identity purely through temperature, never registering as "color."
  • Dark mode is re-decided, not inverted. Alpha borders self-mirror (OpenAI black/12% ↔ white/12%); muted text converges to ≈#818–#9b in every product; shadow opacity multiplies ~3× (Intercom 15%→55%); temperature flips are allowed (Intercom warm→cool, Linear hue 282→272). Claude Console ships no dark mode at all.
