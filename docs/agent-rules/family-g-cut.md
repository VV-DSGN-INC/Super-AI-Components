# Family G and O5 are cut

This rule protects the scope decision that the node builder does not ship.

Family G (G1 to G9), `useFlowRunner` and O5 `flow-shell` are out of the catalog. Do not revive them, and do not restore the moot R2 `run-controls`. The specs and wireframes stay as records, marked cut. The parked `wave-2-flow-foundation` branch is the reference if the decision is ever reversed, and reversing it is Nick's call, not a build task. Where a design came from someone else, as some G designs did, that does not reopen the cut.

The full account: `docs/design-system/decisions.md` D9.

## What it costs

Building a cut item spends a wave on work the product owner has ruled out and puts unsupported components in the registry. The rule prevents that. Following it costs an author nothing beyond checking D9 before picking up an item from an old spec.
