# Data visualization

This registry draws very few charts, on purpose. One component renders a chart, `usage-dashboard`,
through the vendored `chart` wrapper over Recharts; everything else that shows a number is a meter or
a readout. The rules below are therefore short, and two of them are gates: CHT-1 fails the build on
Recharts' default colours, and CHT-3 on its dashed gridline default, because both are the clearest
single tell of an unguided chart.

## Series are a lightness ramp

<Example>
  <div className="flex gap-2">
    <div className="bg-chart-1 border-border size-8 rounded-sm border" />
    <div className="bg-chart-2 border-border size-8 rounded-sm border" />
    <div className="bg-chart-3 border-border size-8 rounded-sm border" />
    <div className="bg-chart-4 border-border size-8 rounded-sm border" />
    <div className="bg-chart-5 border-border size-8 rounded-sm border" />
  </div>
  <p className="text-muted-foreground mt-3 text-xs">chart-1 to chart-5: five greys, light to dark, in both modes</p>
</Example>

Identity across series comes from lightness order and direct labels, never from hue. Assign slots in
fixed order starting at `chart-1` and never cycle; colour follows the entity, so a filter that
removes a series must not repaint the survivors. A sixth series is "Other" or a second chart.
`usage-dashboard` binds its one series to `var(--chart-1)` through a `ChartConfig` and turns on
Recharts' `accessibilityLayer`, which is the shape to copy.

## Most numbers are not charts

<Example className="flex flex-wrap items-start gap-8">
  <div className="w-72">
    <QuotaMeter resources={[
        { label: "Messages", used: 1240, limit: 5000, resetsIn: "Resets in 12 days" },
        { label: "Image generations", used: 34, limit: 200, resetsIn: "Resets in 12 days" }, ]} /></div>
  <div className="flex flex-col gap-2">
    <CreditsIndicator form="ring" balance={640} total={1000} onManage={() => {}} />
    <p className="text-muted-foreground text-xs">the ring form of the cost contract</p>
  </div>
</Example>

The cost contract has three forms, and none of them is a chart: a per-action chip, a pre-commit
line, and this ring. `quota-meter` is a `progressbar` per resource with `aria-valuetext` reading "6 of
30 min used", and the number is always written beside the bar, so the bar never carries the fact
alone. One number is a `stat-readout`, not a chart; twelve numbers with a shape over time are a
chart, not twelve readouts.

## What the vendored wrapper carries

`components/ui/chart.tsx` re-skins Recharts' hardcoded SVG attributes with token classes through
attribute selectors, which is why `check:tokens` reports two hex literals in it: they are match
targets for Recharts' own `stroke="#ccc"`, not colours this system chose. The same class list carries
a `stroke-border/50` gridline, a composite the token system cannot resolve. Both are recorded in
`vendored-token-findings.md` as consumer-facing and not fixed, because fixing them means diverging
from upstream. A chart built here inherits both.

## Rules

- Chart colours are `chart-1` to `chart-5`, in order, never cycled. Recharts' defaults are an
  automatic fail (CHT-1).
- Marks are flat and gridlines are hairline and solid. The dashed `"3 3"` default fails the build
  (CHT-3).
- One axis. Two measures of different scale are two charts, small multiples, or an indexed series.
- Text wears text tokens. Values, labels and legends stay in the foreground tiers; a mark beside
  them carries the identity.
- A legend exists for two or more series and is absent for one; the heading names a single series.
- A single value is a readout or a meter with the number written beside it, never a gauge that
  carries the fact alone.
- Fewer than four points is a readout, not a chart. No pies over a handful of slices, no donut for a
  single value.
- The anti-slop audit's chart lens applies before "done": no gradient fills under lines, no
  oversmoothed curves on sparse data, axes with units and zero-based bars.
