# Motion

Motion here answers one of two questions and never a third: transitions and enter/exit animations
answer _you did something_, looping animation answers _something is in progress_. Nothing loops for
decoration, and the rule is a gate: MOT-1 fails the build on `animate-bounce`, and MOT-2 on
`transition-all`. What is honest to say about the rest is that the registry's reduced-motion guard is
a convention that exists and was then lost across half the tree, which is why this page spends most
of its length on that.

## Durations and curves

The registry names no duration scale. Nearly every transition rides Tailwind's 150ms default, one
site writes `duration-200`, and the marketing tier owns its own keyframes. Curves are Tailwind's
`ease-out` where stated and the default otherwise; overshoot is banned outright, which is what MOT-1's
`animate-bounce` grep enforces. That is a thinner vocabulary than a named scale, and it is stated
here rather than dressed up: a component that needs a longer travel (a sheet, a sidebar collapse)
inherits its timing from the vendored primitive it composes.

## Reduced motion: the convention that was lost

The runner behind `pnpm test:stories` emulates `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` for every story, so
the axe gate always measures the reduced form. A component therefore only _has_ a reduced form if it
branches on the media feature, and two idioms create the branch, both sanctioned by
`story-conventions.md`:

- `motion-reduce:animate-none` beside an `animate-*` class.
- `motion-reduce:transition-none` beside a `transition-*` that a user would perceive as motion: a
  thumb that slides, a panel that grows. A `transition-colors` moves nothing, so suppressing it
  documents no branch.

On a Base UI popup surface the bare guard is inert. `dialog`, `popover`, `tooltip`, `hover-card`,
`dropdown-menu` and `select` animate through `data-open:animate-in`, and Tailwind v4 compiles the
attribute test inside `:where()`, which contributes no specificity, so the tie falls to source order
and the animation wins. The guard has to restate the variant on both halves,
`motion-reduce:data-open:animate-none motion-reduce:data-closed:animate-none`, which sorts after its
counterpart. `sheet` is a third case: it animates by transition, so `motion-reduce:transition-none`
is what suppresses it. `shortcuts-sheet`'s `ReducedMotion` story reads `animationName` back rather
than trusting the class, and that is the assertion to copy.

The state of the tree, counted on 2026-09-04: thirty registry components animate or transition
something a user perceives, and **eleven** carry a guard (`account-menu`, `citation-ref`,
`modality-rail`, `preview-tile`, `pricing-table`, `recommendation-card`, `shortcuts-sheet`,
`sidebar-nav`, `task-tray`, `trace-timeline`, `workspace-switcher`). **Nineteen** do not, including
`generation-queue` and `render-queue`, which are `task-tray`'s
own near-twins and use the identical spinner. Nothing in the pipeline sees it: `check:tokens` reads
colour, and axe does not evaluate the media feature at all. `CONTINUE.md` §9 records it as drift
rather than an open question, and the fix is one class per site, with the restated-variant form on
any popup surface.

## Rules

- Motion answers an action or a wait. Overlays animate on open and close, progress loops, and
  nothing loops for decoration. `animate-bounce` fails the build (MOT-1).
- Name the properties that change: `transition-colors`, `transition-opacity`,
  `transition-transform`. `transition-all` fails the build (MOT-2), because it animates layout by
  accident.
- Use `Skeleton` when the shape of the region is known and `Spinner` when it is not: inside a button,
  as a row glyph, beside a status. `Progress` is the third case, for a wait whose proportion is known.
- Every animation branches on `prefers-reduced-motion`, with the idiom that matches its mechanism:
  `motion-reduce:animate-none` on a spinner or pulse, `motion-reduce:transition-none` on a perceived
  transition, and the restated-variant pair on a Base UI popup.
- A `ReducedMotion` story documents the branch by reading the computed animation back, or documents
  the branch's absence in its description. It never renders identically to its neighbour and implies
  coverage.
- Treat the nineteen unguarded components as known debt, not precedent. Copy `task-tray` for a
  spinner, `preview-tile` for a pulse, `shortcuts-sheet` for a sheet.
- The marketing tier honours reduced motion in every animated component and is the model for a
  component-owned keyframe.

What is being transitioned, the washes and rings a control shifts under the pointer, belongs to
[States](?path=/docs/foundations-states--docs). The runner's emulation is asserted in
[Style axes](?path=/story/foundations-style-axes--reduced-motion-applies).
