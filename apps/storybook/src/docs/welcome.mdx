# Super AI Components

A shadcn-style registry of components for AI applications, distributed by `npx shadcn add` rather
than as an npm package. It is the missing half of [AI Elements](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev): AI
Elements gives you the conversation, this gives you the application around it. As this page renders,
**{systemCounts.catalogItems} catalog items** ship ({systemCounts.primitives} primitives,
{systemCounts.components} components and {systemCounts.blocks} blocks across {systemCounts.families}
families), plus {systemCounts.marketing} marketing mini-components in a second registry namespace and
{systemCounts.libContracts} shared contracts.

This workbench is where the system is read and argued with. Every example on every page is the real
component, imported from the same registry source the consumer install test installs into a fresh
app. Nothing here is a screenshot or a copy.

**The registry is the product.** A component that looks right in this workbench but breaks on
`shadcn add` has failed. That is why the consumer install test is the last step of CI, and why the
numbers above are counted from the tree rather than typed in.

## The layers in the sidebar

All four layers sit on the same shadcn **base-nova** theme (Base UI, not Radix), with the theme and
writing-direction toggles in the toolbar above.

- **Super AI** is the curated registry: the {systemCounts.catalogItems} items this repo authors and
  publishes. Primitives such as `kbd`, `cost-chip` and `entity-row`; components for composing,
  generating, reviewing, monetising and observing; and the shells that compose them into whole
  screens. Every one of them is under the full story-state and documentation contract.
- **AI Elements** is the {systemCounts.aiElements} components of
  [elements.ai-sdk.dev](https://elements.ai-sdk.dev), shown with static mock data. Vendored and
  displayed, never authored here.
- **shadcn** is the {systemCounts.shadcn} primitives in the base-nova style, the foundation everything
  else composes on top of. Also vendored.
- **Marketing** is the {systemCounts.marketing} landing-page mini-components, rebuilt to the same
  token and reduced-motion standards and installed the same way.

```bash
npx shadcn@latest add https://super-ai-components.vercel.app/r/thread-list.json
```

## The rulebook

Foundations and Content in the sidebar are the rulebook. Each page is written from what this repo
actually does, cites the file or gate that makes a claim true, and states what is still a gap. Read
[Principles](?path=/docs/foundations-principles--docs) once; the rest of Foundations applies to every
screen you draw with these parts, and [Voice & principles](?path=/docs/content-voice-principles--docs)
to every word on it.

Two guides describe the edges of the system: [Component
Lifecycle](?path=/docs/guides-component-lifecycle--docs), what "shipped" means here and what is
deliberately absent, and [Browser Support](?path=/docs/guides-browser-support--docs), the floor and
what sets it.

## Status

The catalog is closed: nothing is `building`, and the remaining work is the composition-gap backlog in
`docs/CONTINUE.md` §8. {systemCounts.cut} items were cut from scope in July 2026 (the node builder,
decision D9) and are retained as records rather than deleted. Family P is the second reference board's
first family and is counted separately from the frozen A–O catalog, which is what keeps "closed"
literally true.

Deploys are manual, from `apps/docs`; nothing deploys on merge. The registry is served from
`super-ai-components.vercel.app`.
